Weekly Report

    

      Week = Friday → Thursday. Freezes Thursday 18:00.
      Last week = companies with Last Contact in the finished week.
      Next week plan = open deals with Next Follow-up in the plan week (plus overdue follow-ups).
      Auto-runs Thursday after 18:00 or Friday. You can Generate / Save any day.
